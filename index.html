Juicy Options Screener

A weekly option premium screener for iPhone, iPad, and Mac

What It Does

Every week Juicy Options Screener scans hundreds of stocks and flags the ones whose nearest-the-money option for that week’s Friday expiration is paying worthwhile premium, with a bid/ask spread tight enough to be meaningful. The result is a short, ranked list of names worth a closer look.

It is not a stock picker, it makes no predictions, and it doesn’t do trend analysis. Those judgments stay with you.

Features

Adjustable screen (price band, bid as a percent of strike, bid/ask spread) • Calls, puts, either, or both • Example bear call and bull put spreads found in the option chain data, with net credit, max risk, and return on risk • A return-on-risk grid of every strike combination • 90-day candlestick charts with strike lines • Optional next-earnings dates • Built-in universes (S&P 500, Nasdaq-100, Dow 30, S&P 400 and 600, sector ETFs) and your own custom lists • Bookmarks • Sample data to try the app without an account

Requirements

iPhone or iPad running iOS 26 or later, or a Mac running macOS 26 or later. Live market data needs your own Tradier API token (a free sandbox token works, with delayed data). Earnings dates optionally use a free Alpha Vantage API key.

Read-Only by Design

The app only reads market data. It never places orders, reads account balances or positions, or moves money. Spreads it shows are examples found in the option chain data, not recommendations. Juicy Options Screener is an analysis tool, not investment advice.
