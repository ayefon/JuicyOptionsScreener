Support

Getting started, common questions, and how to get in touch.

Requirements

  • iPhone or iPad running iOS 26 or later, or a Mac running macOS 26 or later
  • A Tradier API token for live market data (a free sandbox token gives delayed data; a production token comes with a Tradier brokerage account)
  • Optional: a free Alpha Vantage API key for next-earnings dates

Getting Started

1. Add your Tradier token

Open Settings → Tradier, choose the Account type that matches your token (Sandbox or Production), and paste the token. It’s stored only in your device’s Keychain.

2. Run a scan

Tap Scan. Matches appear as each option chain is checked, ranked by bid as a percent of strike. The line under the Scan button shows the price range and minimum spread credits the scan will use. Tap a candidate to see its chart, at-the-money options, and example spreads.

3. Not ready for a token yet?

Tap Try with Sample Data on the start screen (or in Settings → Tradier) to explore the whole app with made-up prices.

4. Earnings dates (optional)

Get a free key from Alpha Vantage and add it in Settings → Alpha Vantage.

FAQ

A scan says Tradier rejected the token (HTTP 401)

Check that the Account setting matches the token: sandbox tokens only work with Sandbox, and production tokens only with Production. Use the eye button in Settings to check the saved token for typos.

Earnings dates are blank

Add an Alpha Vantage key in Settings. The free tier allows a limited number of requests per day, so dates can also be blank after many scans in one day.

A symbol never shows up

Share-class tickers use a slash for Tradier (for example BRK/B for BRK.B). Check the symbol’s Tradier form in your custom universe.

Why does a spread say “No qualifying spread”?

No strike in that week’s chain met your target delta and minimum credit. Both are adjustable in Settings → Credit Spreads. In a candidate’s details you can also tap Choose Strikes to start from the strike nearest your target delta.

Can I look at other strikes than the example spread?

Yes. In a candidate’s details, the up and down arrows beside each leg move it one strike, and Shift Both moves both legs together. The numbers and the chart’s strike lines follow. Reset to Example goes back. Each symbol keeps its adjusted strikes while you look at others, until the next scan. Show All Combinations lists the return on risk for every strike pair.

How do I switch between different screen settings?

Save them as a settings set. At the top of Settings, tap Save Current Settings As… to save the Screen and Credit Spreads settings and the selected universes under a name. Load a saved set from Saved Sets; each set’s ⋯ button renames, updates, or deletes it. Sets are saved on your device only.

What are the lines and shaded bands on the chart?

The two blue lines are the 50-day and 200-day moving averages of the daily close; the heavier one is the 200-day. The shaded bands are supply and demand zones found in the price history, with small circles where price has come back to a zone. Turn zones off for one symbol with the switch above the chart, or for every symbol in Settings → Charts. The Daily | Weekly switch shows weekly candles instead.

Can I send the symbols to another app?

Yes. Copy Tickers in the toolbar copies the symbols as one comma-separated list. Share Symbols sends them as a simple CSV file, one symbol per line, for apps that import watchlists.

Does the app place trades?

No. Juicy Options Screener only reads market data. It never places orders, reads account balances or positions, or moves money.
